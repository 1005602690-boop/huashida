常见问题解答FAQ

高周波和超声波有什么区别？HF vs Ultrasonic?+

高周波利用高频电磁场使材料分子极化发热熔接，适合 PVC、皮革等；超声波通过高频机械振动摩擦生热，适合硬胶（ABS/PP 等）。两者适用材料与场景不同。HF uses HF fields to heat molecules — best for PVC/leather. Ultrasonic uses vibration friction — best for rigid plastics (ABS/PP). Different materials & uses.

可以定制模具吗？Can molds be customized?+

支持。提供产品图纸或样品，我们可定制高周波铜/铝模、超声波焊头及电木模，并确认材质与交期。Yes. Send drawings or samples — we customize HF copper/aluminum molds, ultrasonic horns & bakelite molds, with confirmed material & lead time.

设备保修与售后如何？Warranty & service?+

设备提供质保，含安装指导、操作培训与远程技术支持；售后团队响应及时，保障产线稳定运行。Machines come with warranty, install guidance, training & remote support. Our service team responds quickly to keep lines running.

海外客户如何下单与运输？Overseas orders & shipping?+

支持中英双语沟通与出口包装，可协助对接物流与清关资料（如 CE 等资质示意），具体以合同为准。Bilingual service & export packaging; we help with logistics & customs docs (e.g. CE sample). Details per contract.
